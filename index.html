Review areas

What BroGrade looks at

BroGrade is not judging your worth. It is reviewing your presentation: fit, proportions, grooming, styling, photos, and the first changes that would make the biggest visual difference.

Style / outfit fit

Silhouette, proportion, color, and whether the clothes frame you cleanly.

Hair

Shape, length, side bulk, texture, and whether the cut looks intentional.

Grooming

Facial hair, neatness, presentation, and simple cleanup opportunities.

Physique direction

Visual priorities that make clothing sit better and improve your frame.

Photo presence

Lighting, angle, pose, background, and dating-profile read.

Wardrobe direction

The missing pieces that would make your look feel less random.
