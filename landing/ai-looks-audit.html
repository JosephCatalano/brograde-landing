Safe scope

BroGrade reviews presentation, not human worth. It avoids medical, dermatology, mental health, sensitive-trait, and certified fitness advice.

What an AI looks audit should actually do.

A useful AI looks audit should not insult you, flatter you, invent certainty, or guess sensitive traits. It should give a clean read on visible presentation signals and turn them into practical next steps.

BroGrade is designed for men who want direct feedback on the parts they can change: outfit fit, silhouette, haircut direction, facial hair, grooming polish, photos, wardrobe, and visual frame.

Free scan vs full audit.

The free scan gives one quick read: biggest weak point, fastest win, top upgrades, and a mini presentation score. The full audit goes deeper with more photos, a scorecard, barber instructions, shopping priorities, and an execution plan.

When to use BroGrade.

  • You know you can look better, but you cannot tell what is off.
  • Your outfits feel random, basic, or inconsistent.
  • Your dating photos do not match how you look in person.
  • You want a clear upgrade plan before spending money on clothes.
