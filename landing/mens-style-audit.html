Why a style audit works better than guessing.

Most style advice is generic. A photo-based audit is more useful because it starts from your actual proportions, wardrobe, grooming, and target image. The right answer for one guy might be cleaner shoes and straight denim. For another, it might be a haircut, a better jacket length, or removing loud graphics.

The full BroGrade audit gives a scorecard, style and fit breakdown, grooming direction, photo review, shopping list, and 7-day upgrade plan.
