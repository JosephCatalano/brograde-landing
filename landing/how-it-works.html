What BroGrade is for.

BroGrade is for men who want practical appearance feedback without vague compliments or random internet advice. It focuses on presentation: outfit fit, grooming, hair shape, photo presence, wardrobe direction, and general physique direction.

It is not medical advice, mental health advice, dermatology advice, or certified fitness coaching. It is an appearance feedback system built to help you decide what to fix first.
