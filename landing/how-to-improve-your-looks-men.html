Start with the first impression, not random upgrades.

Most men try to improve their looks by buying random clothes, copying a haircut, or changing everything at once. That wastes time because the biggest issue is usually one visible bottleneck: the clothes do not fit, the haircut has no shape, grooming looks unfinished, photos make the person look worse than real life, or the target look is unclear.

BroGrade is built around that bottleneck. It is a private AI-assisted appearance audit for men that turns photos into a style, grooming, physique-direction, photo, and wardrobe upgrade plan.

1. Fix outfit fit before chasing brands.

Better style starts with silhouette. A plain outfit with clean proportions usually beats expensive clothes that collapse at the shoulders, stack at the ankle, or make the frame look smaller.

  • Use tops that create cleaner shoulder and chest structure.
  • Choose pants with the right rise, taper, and break.
  • Keep shoes clean and aligned with the outfit's formality.

2. Make hair and grooming look intentional.

A haircut should frame the face, control side bulk, and match the style you want. Grooming does not need to be complicated. The goal is a clean baseline: facial hair shape, neckline, eyebrows, and basic skin presentation.

3. Improve photo presence.

Bad photos can make a decent look read as low-effort. Use natural light, an eye-level or chest-level camera, a clean background, and a posture that does not collapse the shoulders.

4. Build the visual frame over time.

Physique direction is about visual proportion, not medical or professional fitness advice. For most men, upper chest, shoulders, back, posture, and waist presentation change how clothes sit.

5. Get outside feedback when you cannot see the problem.

If you are too close to your own photos, use a structured review. The free BroGrade Looks Scan gives your biggest weak point and fastest win. The full audit gives the scorecard, haircut direction, shopping priorities, and 7-day plan.
